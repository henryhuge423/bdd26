## Interpretation (frozen scope)

The head converges and fits fold1 near-perfectly (in-sample recall 94–98% at ≈99.9% precision),
yet on fold2 it loses roughly a third of the internal-Dead recall relative to the NP-component
baseline (Δ = −0.34 / −0.35; bootstrap CI entirely negative in both seeds) while slightly
exceeding baseline precision. The threshold-free D2 decoder recovers only part of the gap
(≈0.50 vs 0.75). The operative failure mode of this recipe is the in-sample → out-of-sample
collapse (~98% → ~40% internal-Dead recall) under frozen features, a small conv head, fold1-only
fitting and no augmentation. v3's under-training hypothesis is confirmed (converged loss ~0.10
vs v3 ~1.2) and superseded by this valid negative result: with convergence removed as a
confound, the frozen-feature point head still does not reach — let alone materially exceed —
the existing NP-component proposals. Per the pre-registered rule the point-head line closes at
this budget; a different point head (augmentation, domain randomization, deeper trainable
capacity) would be a separate, separately pre-registered question and is not authorized by
this result.
