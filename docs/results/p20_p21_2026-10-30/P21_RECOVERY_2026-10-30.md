# P21 启动故障与恢复 / Startup failure and recovery

2026-10-30 (America/New_York).

## 原因 / Cause

原数组 `55416129` 的 C19（`_0`）在 37 秒后退出，状态 `FAILED / 5:0`。退出发生在训练前的 `lfs quota` 查询：Lustre 返回退出码 5，并用方括号标记不准确的配额数据。`set -e` 将该查询错误直接当作作业失败。没有训练目录、训练日志或 launch JSON。

The original C19 task failed before training when a Lustre quota query returned exit 5 and explicitly inaccurate totals. Later valid queries showed ample quota. This establishes a transient query failure, rather than evidence of exhausted capacity; the underlying storage-service cause is outside the project.

## 修复 / Fix

新增独立 `scripts/check_lustre_quota.py`：最多查询三次，每次最多 45 秒，间隔 10 秒。只有退出码为零、数据完整且准确、字节和文件配额以及文件系统余量均充足时才放行。配额不足立即失败；不准确数据不会被接受。查询尝试保留 JSONL 诊断。启动时检查 scratch 用户配额、project 用户及组配额；结果保存前再次检查 project 余量。

The recovery wrapper uses bounded, fail-closed quota checks at startup and preservation. It keeps the original sealed training source, data, seeds, model, loss, optimizer, 130 epochs, decoder, final-checkpoint rule, admission criteria, and fixed point-example selection. The original validation remains attached to that unchanged scientific snapshot; the recovery helper and wrapper have separate hashes.

## 验证 / Validation

新增 10 项行为用例先失败，再实现恢复逻辑。首次实现后 27 项通过、1 项失败：测试夹具误将约 47 MiB 余量写成不足 1 MiB；修正夹具为 76 KiB 后，配额和 P21 专项合计 **28 passed in 9.07s**。测试覆盖临时退出码 5 后恢复、三次耗尽、零退出码但不准确的数据、缺失数据、非零退出码但看似有效的数字、字节/文件/soft limit 不足和无限制文件配额。原始失败日志均保留。

Focused checks are appropriate to this storage-only change; metrics, HoVer-Net and training code are unchanged. This is not a new full-suite result. The earlier full P21 suite was 968 passed / 3 skipped. New live Expanse quota, write-readback and scheduler admission evidence is recorded in the recovery receipt.
