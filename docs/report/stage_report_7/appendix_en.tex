Omitting each positive image in turn gives D-minus-C Dead recall changes from $+.016$ to $+.135417$ for seed 19 and $+.032$ to $+.145833$ for seed 1. This is a post-result influence description, not a new confidence interval or replacement for the original gate. The full 11-image differences remain $+.048276$ and $+.068966$.

The original bootstrap samples images containing interior Dead nuclei within each tissue. Four of the seven tissues (Breast, Cervix, Esophagus and Uterus) have only one positive image each, so those single-image groups do not vary under within-tissue resampling. The intervals describe variation available among the existing tissues and images.

The eight predetermined audit examples contain 53/145 Dead nuclei. Matching the same annotated objects across arms gives, for seed 19, 26 retained, 11 gained, six lost and ten missed by both. For seed 1 the counts are 30, eight, four and 11. Each row sums to 53 and per-image hit counts agree with the original saved scores. The sample net gains of five and four differ from the full audit gains of seven and ten. These examples explain specific object transitions; they do not estimate their frequency over the entire cohort.
